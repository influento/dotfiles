Vim cheatsheet · common motions

Common vim motions

leader = Space · [count] op [count] motion · doubled op = whole line

  • 2dawdelete two words
  • ci"change inside quotes
  • d3jdelete three lines down
  • gUiwuppercase inner word
  • dt;delete until ;
  • df)delete through )
  • *cgn …Esc . .rename via search + repeat
  • ggVGselect entire file
  • ddpswap line with next
